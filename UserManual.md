# User Manual
## Introduction
The following manual contains instructions for the setup, usage, and proper maintenance of the Hilliard Smart Christmas Lighting system. 
## Setup
To properly set up the smart lighting system, the following steps must be follow sequentially. If they are done out of order, errors in the function of the system could arise.
1. Plug the Raspberry PI into the wall
   - This must be done for the elements of the system to communicate, and to be able to access the user interface
2. Download the Hilliard Smart Lighting app on any computer with a USB-C port. Plug in the computer or laptop so that the app can run without interruption.
3. Plug in the lighting boxes, and plug in any RGB lights or wall lights into their respective boxes
4. Open the Hilliard Smart Lighting app and press the "Start listening" button
5. Plug in any music playing device to the computer via an aux cord, and plug in any speakers via the USB port (a USB to aux cord may be necessary)
6. Play music
   - At this point the music should play through the speakers, but the lights may not be reacting accordingly
8. Open the following URL: (IP HERE):1880/ui
9. Log in with the credentials
10. Configure all settings
    - When the Raspberry PI is first powered on, all of the settings must be set to something or else the lights might not work. Though it appears that they are set to something, they are not until they have been pressed
    - The lighting boxes may not work until all their settings are set to something
